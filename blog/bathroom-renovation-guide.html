Bathroom Renovation: What to Expect

May 28, 2026 · Smith Construction · 5 min read

A bathroom renovation is one of the best investments you can make in your home. Here's what you can expect when you work with us.

Step 1: Consultation & Design

We start by understanding your vision, measuring your space, and creating a design plan that fits your style and budget.

Step 2: Demolition

Our team carefully removes old fixtures, tile, and materials while protecting the rest of your home from dust and debris.

Step 3: Rough-In Work

Plumbing and electrical updates are completed before any visible work begins, ensuring everything meets code.

Step 4: Installation

New tile, fixtures, vanities, lighting, and accessories are installed with precision and care.

Step 5: Final Walkthrough

We walk through every detail with you to ensure you're 100% satisfied before we consider the job done.

Ready to renovate? Contact us today to get started on your dream bathroom.
